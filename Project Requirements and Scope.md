 ## 1. Project Overview
  The goal for this software assignment is to make a mid to high functioning website with the intention to be a review website for restaurants and food chains to be rated based on comments from their customers. My review site will have the basic requirments for a rating website; customer profile, board for all recent comments, and a 1-5 star raiting system and the ability to ad photos to the review being made.

## 2. Functional Requirments
For my assignment to work it must have a what a standard webpage needs this includes; multiple diffrent buttons to navigate around and to different webpages, this includes the sign up page with the sign in and log in buttons along with the create a post button that lets you share your comment to the app, and a button that brings you to your profile or others profiles by tapping on said persons username or your profile in the courner that lets you see prevous comments on other resteraunts. Another function that is simular  to the buttons is the (likey possibility) of a star raiting system along with a photo function, these functions help to back up the review and give people a summary of what you thought of the resteraunt, the stars will have the possibility of doing a full star or half a star by clicking on the buttons underneath the comment box and they will start out grey giving the option to give zero stars or turn yellow when clicked. The photo function has the option to add 1-5 photos of your meal plus anything else that may need to be added eg. how clean the environment may be, this function helps back up your comment made for the resteraunt. 
Other functions required include; not being able to edit your own post after posting it or others posts that are made to keep reviews honest and make sure no one is trying to change reviews to make one company seem good, there will also be the funtion to not be able to make a comment without having an account made, you can view reviews on the review pages but in order to make a comment on a resteraunt you must make an account.

## 3. Non-functional requirments
Non-functional requirments include: How fast the website is, wether this be loading time between the webpages, how fast it is to post a review or signing up to the app the speed must be of good quality otherwise people will loose interest with the app due to its slow preformance as no one wants to wait ages just to make a comment. Security is another important requirment that is needed, when creating a password or re-entering a password it must show up blured so that no one can access someones account while watching them put in the password, with the option to show password via button, another important step is saving peoples account information with a security code only known by me and the program that encripts the email and password so if the app is ever hacked it doesnt put the persons sensitive information at risk.
