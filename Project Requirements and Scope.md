## 1. Project Overview
  The goal for this software assignment is to make a mid to high functioning website with the intention to be a review website for restaurants and food chains to be rated based on comments from their customers. My review site will have the basic requirments for a rating website; customer profile, board for all recent comments, and a 1-5 star raiting system and the ability to ad photos to the review being made.
